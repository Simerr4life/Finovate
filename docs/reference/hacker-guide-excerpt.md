## Submissions



**Hacking ends at 9:00 AM on SUNDAY OCTOBER 11th. ALL MATERIALS MUST BE SUBMITTED BY THE END OF HACKING TIME. NO EXCEPTIONS!**


All projects must be submitted through [**Devpost**](https://hacklanta.devpost.com/).

Teams may submit to only **ONE** main track and a **MAXIMUM of 3** side challenges. 

## Requirements

### 🛠️ What to Build

- An original, working project built during the official Hacklanta hacking period.
- Software, hardware, AI, dev tools, consumer, fintech, creative tech, or any technical build that fits a track or challenge.
- Open-source libraries, APIs, frameworks, AI tools, and starter templates are allowed. **Disclose anything that existed before Hacklanta.**
- The main design, development, and integration must happen during the event.
- Previously completed projects, projects reused from another hackathon without major new work, or projects that misrepresent how they were built are **not eligible**.

### 📦 What to Submit

- Project title and description
- The problem you are solving and who it serves
- Team roster (**max 4 people**)
- Your main track and any side challenges
- Public GitHub repo with a useful README
- Working demo link or clear run instructions
- Short demo video of the project in action
- Technologies, APIs, datasets, and sponsor tools used
- Disclosure of anything made before Hacklanta
- Any test credentials or special instructions for judges

**All links must stay accessible throughout judging.**

## Submission Instructions

Your Devpost submission should have ALL of the following:

### 1. Project Write-Up

The brief project write-up should outline your ideation and development process. This description should be listed on the Devpost submission itself.

**Judges will be asking themselves these types of questions to judge your project [NOTE: These are just sample questions to help you out]:**

### 2. 2-3 Minute Demo Video

Your video should highlight the main features of your project and explain your project.

This can be submitted as a URL to a YouTube/Google Drive video, etc.

MAKE SURE YOUR VIDEO IS LISTED AS "PUBLIC".

### 3. Link to GitHub Repository

The link to your GitHub repository should contain all the code for your project, with a README. MAKE SURE YOUR GITHUB REPO IS LISTED AS "PUBLIC".

You must also submit a Google Form containing your Devpost submission link, project name, team members' details, your chosen track, and your chosen challenge(s). **This form will be available to all teams before the submission time (check Discord announcements).**

> You MUST submit this form and upload your project on Devpost for your project to be considered.
> 

Only ONE member from each team should submit the project on Devpost as well as the Google Form.

# 🧑‍⚖️ Judging


**Judging Expo: Sunday, October 11th, 9:30 AM - 11:30 AM (Student Center East State Ballroom)**


- Does the project address the track's prompt?
- How polished is the prototype or demo?
- How effective is the team's presentation?
- Is there a clear, compelling use case?

## Procedure for Judging:

You will have:

- 3 minutes to pitch your project
- 2 minutes for Q&A

In the judging room, there will be desks with 2 judges each. You will wait in line until a judging table is open and you will present your project! You will know when a judging table is open when the Hacklanta staff nearby call you over. After presenting, please exit the room for steady flow (follow the Hacklanta staff). The closing ceremony will be hosted shortly after deliberation is over (12:45 PM in the State Ballroom).

# 🎚️ Points and Redeeming

Every Hacklanta activity is worth **points**. Scan the activity's QR code with your account on the members platform to earn them, then spend them at the **prize shop**.

1. Check in to Hacklanta first (this unlocks everything else)
2. Scan the QR at each activity you attend
3. Win competitions and staff will scan **your** personal QR
4. Trade your points for swag at the prize shop

☝

**Good to know:**

- Each activity can only be scanned **once**. Repeat scans show "already checked in"
- Workshop QRs only open **near the end**, so stay to get credit
- Only staff can redeem prizes. Points come off when they scan you
